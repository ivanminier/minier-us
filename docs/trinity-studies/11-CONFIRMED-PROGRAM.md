# Trinity Peaks: confirmed program decisions (Ivan Minier, 6 Nov 2026)

These are owner decisions. Drawings must follow them; change them only on Ivan's instruction, and log the change.

| # | Decision (confirmed) |
|---|---|
| 1 | The building in the brief is **Trinity Peaks**. |
| 2 | **3 RA rooms per floor in each building** (6 per floor across North and South): one is the 9th bedroom in the front block, one is in each tower. |
| 3 | **Front** = the side facing the dining entrance and lobbies, where the ground floor is partly buried. The **8 Jack-and-Jill singles (4 shared baths) + 1 RA** sit at the head of each building's main tower, above its lobby. |
| 4 | **Middle towers are straight** (they face each other across the courtyard and bridges): narrower communal-style rooms with ungendered pod bathrooms. **Outer towers are L-shaped** (they face the site edges): larger rooms with attached bathrooms, through a suite or private. |
| 5 | **End-of-floor blocks, floors 2–5:** 4 private bedrooms around a living room, 2 bathrooms, no kitchen, about 900–1,100 sf. |
| 6 | **Designed triples only:** generous (about 260–300 sf), chosen, lower rate, capped near 5% of beds, never overflow. **Used mainly in odd-shaped ends and in lofted rooms with spiral staircases.** |
| 7 | **Floor 1:** 2 live-in staff/faculty apartments per building (4 total), plus residential life offices and the lower level of the amenity stack. |
| 8 | **Bed target:** 1,450–1,600 across both buildings; the drawings report what fits. |
| 9 | **Two height tracks:** the preferred design (ground + 6 + loft, about 85–95 ft, **requires a zoning amendment**) and a ≤55-ft compliant alternative. The preferred concept is not compromised to fit 55 ft. |
| 10 | **No numbers from UVM drawings are published.** Public text says only "informed by University Heights and CCRH typologies". |

Still open (not blocking Round 1): number of two-storey loft rooms and how many get the elevator-level entry; which rooms get setback balconies; whether the bridge holds only circulation or also a lounge.
