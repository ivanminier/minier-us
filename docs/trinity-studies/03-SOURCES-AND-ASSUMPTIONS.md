## Assumptions that are not sourced (must be replaced before reliance)

- **All plan dimensions** (`E-*` in `studies/assumptions.json`): room sizes, wall weights, door widths, suite depth 28 ft, apartment depth 26 ft, corridor 6 ft, stair and elevator blocks.
- **Height arithmetic:** ground 15 ft, residential 10–11 ft, roof 6 ft. The CDO height-measurement method is not analysed.
- **Finance:** premium increment, contingency, hard/soft split, construction months, draw curve, all interest rates, debt shares, lease months, occupancy, other income, opex, tax/PILOT, DSCR and yield targets, cap rates. Scenario B rent is **blank** by design. The model solves for required rent instead.
- **Test-fit structure:** a 252-ft bar as a floor-plate "equivalent", all-four-bedroom Rise, 30-ft core zone.

## Missing inputs (requested in the final report)

UVM designed-bed counts for McAuley and Mercy; headcounts by class year; triple counts; boundary and topographic survey; inventory of retained buildings; a planner's reading of the overlay; structural grid and floor heights; surveyed comparable rents; professional cost input; confirmation of who performed the Oct 29, 2026 audit and of AI use in Trinity research and writing.
