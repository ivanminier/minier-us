## 3. How we iterate (the workflow you asked for)

Each **round** produces one small, reviewable packet:

1. **Plan sheets.** Each option is drawn large and legibly (PNG + PDF), with enlarged callouts of key rooms.
2. **Decision card per option.** It covers:
   - what I did and why
   - what it costs (area, beds, bathrooms)
   - which of your brief points it satisfies
   - what I *invented* and needs your call
   - the benchmark comparison (rounded University Heights and CCRH aggregates)
3. **Checks.** Automatic geometry checks, plus a **manual usability checklist** (desk-chair clearance, bed access, door approach, kitchen aisles, accessible unit present, daylight to every bedroom). Each item is marked "verified by checker", "verified by review", or "not verified".
4. **Your feedback** in numbered lines, for example "Option B: make J&J baths 2 ft wider; move RA to the corner." I apply it as the next **version**. Earlier versions stay in history, and the changelog records whose decision each change was.

I won't start the next round until you reply. If you'd like, I can host each round's packet as a **private review page** (an Artifact on claude.ai) where you leave comments directly on each option. Say so and I'll set it up. Otherwise packets come as files.

## 5. Technical work behind the rounds (no rewrite)

The existing generator, checker, exports, and site pages stay. Scoped additions:
- **Wing frames:** place rectangular room layouts in a rotated wing frame, for angled and L-shaped towers. Checks stay in each wing's local frame.
- **Clearance zones:** desk-chair zone, bed-side access, door approach, and kitchen work aisle, checked like the furniture overlaps.
- **Structural grid** overlay, and a check that demising walls fall on it.
- **Floor stacks:** a building spec that lists floors, each assembled from room types, with automatic bed and area totals.
- **Massing export:** extruded geometry (SVG axonometric plus a simple 3D file) with heights computed from floor-to-floor assumptions.
- **Model restructure** (finance): A1 system-financed, A2 project test, B P3/private.
