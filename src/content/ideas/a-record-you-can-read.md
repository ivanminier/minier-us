---
title: A record you can read six months later
date: "2026-11-23"
summary: I built Rental Record around a better question than how many photos you took.
project: rental-record
---

A move-in photo makes sense on the day you take it. Six months later, it can be hard to remember whether that close-up was taken in the kitchen or the entryway, let alone what you were trying to document. I wanted to give each photo enough context to make sense later.

In Rental Record, the room comes first. A wide photo establishes where you are. A close-up shows what you saw. A written observation adds the detail the photo can't carry. Keys, meter readings, and access devices belong to the handoff, where they're easy to find when you move out.

I think about the PDF as the last screen of the app. Someone opening it should be able to follow the rooms in the same order as the walkthrough and understand what each photo is there to show. Working backward from that reader helped me decide what belongs in the inspection flow.
