---
title: Messy catalogs, calm television
date: "2026-08-14"
summary: Lineup started as a player problem and became an organization problem.
---

A raw IPTV catalog is usually not television. It is a list: duplicated names, leftover categories, missing logos, and a guide that may or may not belong to the channel you think you are watching.

Lineup’s job is to leave that raw list intact and derive something a person can actually sit down with. The interesting work is not the play button. It is the translation.

This is a note about a product that is still in development, not a launch announcement.
