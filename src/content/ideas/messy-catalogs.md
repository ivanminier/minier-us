---
title: The catalog is the product
date: "2026-08-14"
summary: Lineup is a player. The harder work is turning a provider list into something you can actually watch.
---

A raw IPTV catalog is usually not television. It is a list: duplicated names, leftover categories, missing logos, and a guide that may or may not belong to the channel on screen.

Lineup keeps that raw list intact. It derives names, categories, logos, order, and guide matches. Playback is expected. Organization is the product.

This is a note about software that is still in development, not a launch announcement.
