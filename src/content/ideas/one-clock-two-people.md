---
title: One clock, two people
date: "2026-11-25"
summary: Same Minute started with the small problem of doing Pomodoros with my mom from another city.
project: same-minute
---

My mom and I wanted to work alongside each other from different cities. Starting two timers at about the same time was easy. It got awkward if one of us joined late or wanted to pause.

In Together mode, both screens count down to the same end time. When you join late, you see the time that's actually left. Either person can pause a shared session, and the controls tell you when an action will affect both people. If you lose the connection, the app marks the countdown as last known and holds the shared controls until you reconnect.

I also wanted Same Minute to be worth opening when nobody else was free. The solo timer works offline as soon as you open it. When a focus interval ends, the app offers a break and waits for you to start it. That pause felt important for the kind of time I wanted to spend together.
