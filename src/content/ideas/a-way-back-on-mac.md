---
title: A workspace switch needs a way back
date: "2026-11-05"
summary: Designing Modeboard meant thinking about what happens after someone presses Apply.
project: modeboard
---

I wanted a study setup and a work setup to feel different on the same Mac. A Modeboard profile could put the right apps, files, and Dock items within reach. But once an app changes the desktop you already know, a fast switch is only part of the job.

The profile editor shows the settings you can change before you apply them. A backup gives you a way back for supported settings, and the restore history is visible in the app. I put that recovery flow where people could find it without remembering a keyboard shortcut or looking up a support article.

The same idea guided the Dock pin editor. Dragging something into place is easy to understand when the order you see is the order you are editing. I wanted each small change to feel deliberate, especially when several of them are bundled into a single profile.
