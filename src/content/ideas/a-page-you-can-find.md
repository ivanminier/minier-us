title: A page you can find
date: "2026-11-27"
summary: Giving each campus and residence hall a page someone can find without knowing the name LaundryLink.
project: uvm-laundrylink
---

Someone looking for laundry in Wright Hall may search for exactly that. They have no reason to start with the name of the app. The original tracker opened rooms inside a single JavaScript interface, which worked once someone had arrived and found the directory.

I added pages for campuses, halls, and complexes that a browser can read before the app starts. Those pages explain which rooms are available to look up and link directly to the tracker for each room. They use the same room mapping as the app, so a correction to a floor or hall name can carry through to both views.

That work also made me look more closely at the route from a search result to a room. Useful link names, keyboard access, and a predictable way back through the directory matter on a first visit. Finding your way to the right room is part of what the app has to do.
