---
title: What I need a feasibility study to answer
date: "2026-11-10"
summary: A site model helped me see which parts of the Trinity proposal needed better questions.
project: trinity-redistricting
---

While revising Trinity in November, I kept coming back to the people who already live there. On a plan it is easy to mark a building for replacement. A student in that building still needs somewhere to sleep, eat, and get to class while the work happens. That is why the phasing now starts with opening part of Trinity Peaks before replacing McAuley and Mercy, and keeps the Back Five open until a later phase.

The exterior views helped me talk about how the district could feel. Working with a Blender site model brought a different set of questions into view. Where does the slope make an entrance difficult? How much room is actually available for buildings and the paths between them? How would service and emergency access work?

Those questions now matter more to me than another polished image. The proposal asks for help scoping a feasibility study, including a way for current residents to weigh in. I want enough information to decide which parts of the idea deserve to move forward, and which ones should change.
