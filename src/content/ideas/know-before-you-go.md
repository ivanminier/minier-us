---
title: Know before you go
date: "2026-11-27"
summary: Why the room you use every week should be the easiest one to find.
project: uvm-laundrylink
---

If the laundry room is a few floors away, finding out all the washers are in use means carrying everything back upstairs. The question I wanted LaundryLink to answer was whether it was worth making the trip yet.

That gave me a fairly simple way to decide what belonged on the first screen. It needed a room list, a search field, and familiar campus names. A student in Central Campus Residence Hall may look for “CCRH” and a floor number. A student in Wright Hall may know the name of the hall and never need to think about the name of the complex it belongs to. Both should be able to find their room.

I added favorites because that same room matters the next week, too. Once someone has found the room they use, there’s little value in asking them to look it up from scratch. The directory helps the first time. A saved room makes the next visit shorter.
